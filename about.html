About MECR

MECR (Multi‑Defendant Conflict Resolver) is an AI‑driven platform that analyses complex legal cases involving multiple defendants and pieces of evidence. Our system combines state‑of‑the‑art natural‑language processing, graph‑based reasoning, and explainable AI to surface contradictions and provide clear, actionable reports for legal professionals.

Our Mission

To empower lawyers, judges, and researchers with intelligent tools that reduce manual effort, increase accuracy, and promote transparency in the judicial process.
